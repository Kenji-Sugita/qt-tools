# DECISIONS

## Accepted

### 1. Policy Name

- Describe the adopted policy.
- Briefly explain why it was chosen.

## Rejected

### 1. Alternative Name

- Describe the alternative considered.
- Explain why it was not adopted.
- State the conditions for reconsideration.

## Undecided

### 1. Question Name

- Describe an unresolved matter requiring a decision, rather than an implementation task.
- State what to consult before deciding.
- Put work whose start date alone is deferred in `backlog.md`.

## Decision Criteria

- State what to prioritize when deciding.
- Example: Do not break existing URLs.
- Example: Keep the home page from growing too large.

## Lasting Specifications and Operating Constraints

- Describe specifications to preserve in future changes.
- Describe operating constraints to observe during work and their rationale.
