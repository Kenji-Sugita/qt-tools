# NEXT

Before reading or updating this file, read `~/AGENTS.next.md` and follow its instructions.
If `~/AGENTS.next.md` does not exist, do not reorganize or delete this file; ask the user first.
Do not change this rule unless the user explicitly instructs you to do so.
After copying this template to its destination, replace its contents with the minimum information needed to resume work next time.

## Current State

- Describe the purpose of the work.
- State what is complete and what remains.
- Describe the current position.
- Limit detailed work history and validation logs to what is needed for the next decision.

## Next Actions

- State what to do first next time.
- Normally limit this to one or two items.
- Even with multiple topics, include only the next step for each.

## Incomplete Items

- Include only incomplete items needed for the next work decision.
- Move detailed remaining work, problems, and deferred items to `backlog.md`.
- Move information to retain as decisions or operating constraints to `DECISIONS.md`.

## Files to Change

- List the files you expect to change next time and why.
- Example: `index.html`: Check the hero display.

## Notes

- Record constraints, accident-prevention measures, pending approvals, and fragile areas to recall immediately next time.
- Example: Do not change existing URLs.

## Related Files

- List files to consult when resuming and explain their roles.
- `DECISIONS.md`: Lasting decisions, specifications, and operating constraints.
- `backlog.md`: Remaining work, problems, and deferred items not to be tackled immediately next time.
